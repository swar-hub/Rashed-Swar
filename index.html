Rashed Swar

Bahraini Visual Artist

Rashed Swar is a Bahraini Visual artist who creates art using his environment and whatever materials are available at his disposal.

About

Rashed Swar was a pioneering Bahraini artist whose career spanned 35 years until his passing in 1986. Known for his emotional brushwork and timeless representations of Bahraini life and Culture, His art captured the essence of traditional Middle Eastern heritage.
